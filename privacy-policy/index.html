Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Who we are

Merit Education Group ("MEG", "we", "us") produces high school mathematics curriculum for Minnesota classrooms. This policy explains what information we collect throughmeriteducationgroup.com and the small number of services we use to run it. If you have questions, contact us at raymond.diaz@meriteducationgroup.com or (612) 562-8584.

Information you give us

When you email us, call us, request a sample lesson, schedule a walkthrough, or submit a form on this site, we receive the information you provide. That is normally your name, your email address, your phone number if you share it, your school or district, and the content of your message. We use it only to answer you, to provide samples and quotes, and to deliver materials you ask for.

Information collected automatically

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set advertising cookies, we do not run third-party trackers, and we do not build profiles of visitors. Our host records ordinary web server information, including IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and reliability. Our site's font and page assets are served from the same host.

Student information

We do not collect student information through this website, and we do not ask visitors to submit it. Curriculum is delivered to districts under a written agreement, which governs any student data involved in delivery. We follow the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and applicable Minnesota law for any district data we handle, and we do not use district or student data for marketing or resale.

How we produce curriculum

We produce, review, and deliver curriculum using Google Workspace, including Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides, with access limited to MEG staff accounts. Internal production tools authorized by us may access only our own curriculum files in our own Google Drive, using permissions granted by the MEG account owner, and that access is used solely to create, organize, and deliver MEG curriculum.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our website host and Google Workspace), with a district under an agreement, or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep contact and business records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete them. We use access controls, encryption in transit, and vendor security features such as Google Workspace access controls to protect information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for educators, administrators, and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it. If you believe a child has provided information through this site, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Write to raymond.diaz@meriteducationgroup.com to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, to ask us to delete it, or to ask us to stop contacting you. We will respond within a reasonable time and honor requests as the law allows.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes affecting how we use information you already gave us will be described here.
