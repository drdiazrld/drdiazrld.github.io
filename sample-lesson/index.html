Lesson

U1L01 — What Is a Variable?

Duration

50 minutes (adaptable to 90-minute block)

MN Standards

MN 9.3.5.3, MN 9.3.5.7, MN 9.3.5.8

Career Connection

Medical Lab Scientist — Mayo Clinic

Objective

Define a variable and distinguish it from constants and coefficients. Identify variables in algebraic expressions and write one-variable expressions to model quantitative situations.

Key Vocabulary

variableexpressionconstantcoefficient

Enduring Understanding

Variables are symbols that represent unknown or changing quantities — they let us generalize relationships instead of computing single answers.

Direct Instruction (I Do) — 6 min

Teacher Script:

Variable: A letter or symbol that represents an unknown quantity or a value that changes within a given context. Variables allow us to generalize relationships rather than compute single numerical answers.

Expression: A mathematical phrase composed of numbers, variables, and operation symbols. Expressions do not contain equality or inequality signs.

Constant: A fixed numerical value in an expression that does not change.

Coefficient: The multiplicative factor placed directly before a variable term. If no number is written, the coefficient is implicitly 1.

Precision Language

"We do not say 'x times n.' We state: 'the product of a constant and a variable' or use proper notation."

Culture & Career Spotlight — 6 min

Anishinaabe Basket Weaving

Traditional weavers use 8 + r to model total reeds, where r captures how material needs scale with design complexity.

Career: Medical Lab Scientist — $63,150/yr

A medical lab scientist at Mayo Clinic, Rochester MN — the salary comes from MN DEED OES 2026 Q1 data (25th percentile × 2080 hrs), verified against O*NET. s × h + 5 models total tasks as samples per hour, hours worked, and quality checks. Variables enable career modeling rather than static calculations.

Guided Practice (We Do) — 8 min

Example 1: Identification

7k - 4

Identify the variable, constant, and coefficient.

Variable: k | Constant: -4 | Coefficient: 7

Example 2: Translation

Write an expression for "5 less than three times a number."

Solution: Let n represent the unknown number. 3n - 5

Example 3: Real-World Model

A community garden charges a flat fee of $12 and $4 per plot rented. Write an expression for total cost if p plots are rented.

Solution: 12 + 4p

GEMDAS Reminder: When you substitute values for variables, apply order of operations (Grouping, Exponents, Multiplication/Division, Addition/Subtraction) strictly.

Independent Worksheet — 18 min (9 work + 9 circulate)

T1 — Foundational

Identify variables/constants/coefficients in given expressions; match verbal phrases to one-variable expressions.

T2 — Application

Write one-variable expressions from multi-step verbal descriptions and real-world scenarios.

T3 — Extension

Determine if two expressions are equivalent when variables are assigned specific values; justify using substitution.

Common Misconceptions & Redirect Strategies

MisconceptionRedirect QuestionStrategy
Confuses variable with constant"Which part changes depending on the situation?"Color-code: variables blue, constants black
Writes 5 × n instead of 5n"How do mathematicians abbreviate multiplication?"Model implicit multiplication; emphasize coefficient placement
Translates "3 less than x" as 3 − x"Read carefully — what is being decreased?"Arrow diagrams to reverse operation order

Exit Ticket — 2 min

1. Define a variable in your own words using precise mathematical language.

2. Write an algebraic expression for: "Twice a number decreased by 9." Let n represent the unknown number.

Formative assessment directly tied to the lesson objective. Word-problem readiness check.
