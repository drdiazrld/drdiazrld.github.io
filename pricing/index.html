Questions Districts Ask

Is it really one-time? No renewals, ever?+

Yes. The district pays a one-time setup fee and a one-time per-student price, buys the course outright, and owns it forever. There are no subscriptions, no annual fees, no renewals — the price you see is the price you pay, once.

How do you price a district at $25 per student?+

Simple: a one-time $5,000 setup per course, plus your high-school enrollment × $25. A district with 750 students pays $5,000 + $18,750 = $23,750 per course — or $15,000 + $56,250 = $71,250 for all three — one time. The setup fee is identical for every district and covers onboarding, LMS integration, and staff training. No bands, no enrollment floors, no caps.

What exactly does a course include?+

140+ ready-to-teach slide decks, lesson plans and teacher editions, guided notes, worksheets (with Spanish versions), bell ringers, exit tickets, quizzes, end-of-unit assessments, QTI exports, PBIS game masters, slide-synced substitute plans, family letters in English (additional languages on request), answer keys, reference materials, and built-in teacher training — everything a teacher needs to walk in and teach.

How does this compare to free options?+

Free open-source materials exist, and they're worth acknowledging. What they don't include is the ready-to-teach Minnesota-aligned course: complete decks, lesson plans, and assessments built for your classrooms. We're not the cheapest option — we're the best value per dollar for a curriculum that's truly ready.

What about the Personal Finance course?+

MEG's Personal Finance course follows the same pricing — a one-time $5,000 setup, plus $25 per student, one time. It's built to meet Minnesota's Personal Finance graduation requirement — required for every student entering 9th grade in 2024–25 and later — so it's one less course your district has to build.

Can we pilot the curriculum before committing district-wide?+

Absolutely. A single-school pilot is a great way to run the full program at one site and evaluate the results before expanding. We also provide a complete sample unit at no cost during the evaluation phase so your curriculum committee can thoroughly review the materials.
