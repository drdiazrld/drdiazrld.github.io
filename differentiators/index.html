Not All Algebra Curricula Are Created Equal

MEG Algebra 1, Algebra 2, and Geometry were built from the ground up for Minnesota classrooms. Every decision — from the problems we write to the way we structure class time — was made with Minnesota students and teachers in mind. Here's what makes us different.

Culturally & Tribally Responsive

Minnesota has a rich cultural heritage, including 11 sovereign Dakota and Ojibwe nations. Our curriculum brings Indigenous cultural contexts into mathematical problems — not as an afterthought, but as an integral part of the learning experience, with facts verified against primary sources.
