Designed for Every Learner

How MEG Supports Every Learner

Our curriculum isn't one-size-fits-all. Every lesson is built on four pillars that ensure every student — regardless of background or skill level — can access and succeed in Intermediate Algebra, Algebra 2, and Geometry.

Career Connections

Every lesson ties algebra to real careers — civil engineering, healthcare, construction, IT, trades — so students see where math leads and why it matters.

Cultural Relevance

Dakota and Ojibwe cultural contexts are featured in problems, scenarios, and examples — with facts verified against primary sources. Students see their heritage and community in the math they do every day.

Multiple Representations

Concepts are taught through graphs, tables, equations, and words — because every student learns differently. Multiple entry points mean no one gets left behind.

Scaffolded Practice

Every problem set builds from guided practice to independent work. Students gain confidence step by step — no more jumping into deep water without support.
